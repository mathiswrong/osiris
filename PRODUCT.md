# KNUCKLETAT

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Explore open source intelligence by locale. Start with flashpoints on the map, select a region, and examine its reporting, sources, public-channel leads, broadcasts, and geographic context together.

## Operating Context
The map is the home screen. Zooming out reveals the globe, then satellite orbits; zooming in reverses that path. Regional desks are the detailed investigation destination. Satellite and source-health workspaces remain available through navigation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Live publisher reports and provider observations come from existing connectors. Telegram previews are sampled public posts. Private WhatsApp feeds are not currently connected. Named regions are contextual locations, not inferred incident coordinates. Publisher repetition does not establish independent verification. Report examples in a design brief must not be fabricated as live events.

## Product Principles
- Locale comes before source or topic.
- Display watch status consistently in map filters and report tags.
- Keep original evidence and source attribution accessible.
- Distinguish measured observations from unverified reporting.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the KNUCKLETAT name and logo. Use Inter for reading and controls; reserve Courier for data, counts and timestamps. Build directly in the existing web interface. The regional desk should provide an advanced, organized view of everything available about a selected place.

## Open Decisions
Primary audience and any future private-channel integrations are not specified.
