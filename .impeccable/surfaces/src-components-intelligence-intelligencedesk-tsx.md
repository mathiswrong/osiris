# Map and regional intelligence

Mode: Operate. Scope: home map, globe and orbital view and regional desk. Keep existing data providers, logo, day/night themes, evidence and review actions.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Place is the entry point. Flashpoint cards belong to geographic anchors; regional evidence belongs to one focused desk.
OWN-WORLD: User-pinned news-wire simplicity inspired by Drudge Report: readable Inter typography for prose and controls, with native Courier reserved for data, counts and timestamps, white daylight and charcoal night surfaces, square controls, strong underlined headlines, and the existing illustrated logo. Amber-to-red map markers encode 1–3, 4–9, and 10+ reports in the displayed watch's 24h window; red also flags measured alerts and reporting surges, each explicitly labeled. Volume does not establish severity or verification. Watch-status tags remain a separate labeled dimension: red emerging, brown early, burnt orange multi-source, green verified observations, grey long term.
STORY: Scan the map, zoom out through the globe to satellite orbits, filter watch status, select a locale, compare reporting and public leads, inspect original evidence.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact header and workspace navigation above a full-width map. Short leader lines connect place markers to headline cards. Regional desks lead with a location header, a focused situation map, regional broadcast context, and a source-filtered stream.
FORM: User-pinned map-first organization, with globe and satellite views revealed by zooming out; seed key: brief-pinned. Signature interaction: anchored headline callouts reveal a regional desk with matching status tags.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
